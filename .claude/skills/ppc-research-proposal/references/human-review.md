# Human review gate

The workflow produces research and a recommendation. A person at the agency makes the commercial decisions and approves the client recommendation when evidence is uncertain. Keep the two visibly separate.

## What the workflow may produce on its own
Search demand and clusters, keyword lists, CPC and competition ranges, market and competitor observations (verified or marked unverified), landing-page observations, coverage math and the entry-budget analysis, media allocation logic among clusters, measurement framing tied to the prospect's objective, regulated-industry flags, draft proposal text, and a draft reply to the prospect.

## What a human must decide (never invent; use highlighted placeholders)
Management fee, setup fee, pricing structure; contract length, cancellation terms, proposal validity; conflict-of-interest answer; market exclusivity position; any guarantee, legal promise, or strategic commitment; the final client recommendation when the evidence is provisional; whether to send at all.

## How to present it
- In the proposal: bracketed, highlighted placeholders with a tag ([AGENCY TO SET], [AGENCY TO CONFIRM], [AGENCY TO DECIDE]) and, where useful, two alternative drafts the reviewer can choose between (for example a no-conflict statement and a disclosed-conflict statement). Never a number the agency did not supply.
- In the internal note: a review checklist listing each decision, the options, and the trade-off in one line each (for example, exclusivity across a whole category blocks future clients in that category).
- Uncertain evidence: say which figures are provisional and what would firm them up, so the reviewer can decide whether to send now or after the export.

## Before sending
The reviewer confirms: placeholders resolved or consciously left for the prospect call; provisional labels accurate; no claim about the prospect or competitors that the research did not verify; no hidden party named anywhere, including document metadata; regulated-industry flags appropriate for the vertical.
