# Compliance modules: other verticals

Use the healthcare module as the pattern: Agent C verifies each item as of the run date with citations, records owners, and the orchestrator holds the defaults. For a vertical not listed, build the list from the same five questions: (1) which Google Ads policy applies to the category (certifications, restricted claims, sensitive categories); (2) which federal rules govern advertising, testimonials, and tracking (FTC Endorsement Guides 2023, the 2024 reviews rule, sector regulators); (3) which state rules govern the profession's advertising and the state's privacy law; (4) which data is sensitive enough to keep out of ad platforms; (5) which trademarks and names need permission.

## Legal services
- Google Ads: legal services are a restricted category in some regions; check the Legal services policy and local certification requirements (for example bail bonds and lawsuit funding restrictions).
- Bar rules: the state's Rules of Professional Conduct 7.1 to 7.5 (no misleading communications, no unjustified expectations, "specialist" claims only with certification, required disclaimers such as "advertising material", trade name rules, fee advertising). Client testimonials and "best lawyer" claims are constrained in many states.
- Privacy: prospective-client inquiries are confidential; form contents and call recordings stay in firm systems; no remarketing on legal problems (sensitive category under Google's personalized advertising policy: "personal hardships").
- Terms: no fee guarantees, no outcome promises ("we win"), conflict checks before intake.

## Dental and orthodontics
Same as healthcare (HIPAA covered), plus: Google's rules on before-and-after imagery in some placements; state dental board advertising rules; discount and financing claims (Truth in Lending disclosures for financing offers).

## Home services (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, remodeling)
- Google Ads: Local Services Ads certification exists as a separate product; search ads have no certification but Google's misrepresentation policy applies to pricing claims; state contractor license numbers may be required in ads (California, Arizona, and others).
- Testimonials and reviews: FTC rules; no fake or incentivized reviews without disclosure.
- Privacy: no sensitive categories; standard consent for call recording under state all-party consent laws (California, Florida, Illinois, Washington, and others) applies to call tracking recording.

## Financial services and insurance
- Google Ads financial products and services policy: certification or verification requirements vary by product and country (debt services, loans, crypto, insurance); check the current certification list.
- Regulators: CFPB and UDAAP, state insurance advertising rules, FINRA rules for broker-dealers (retail communications), Truth in Lending and Truth in Savings for rate claims.
- Privacy: GLBA safeguards; financial hardship is a sensitive personalized-advertising category (no remarketing on debt or bankruptcy interests).

## Education and career training
- Google Ads: education policy; some programs restricted; no misleading job-placement or salary claims (FTC actions and state AG cases).
- Privacy: FERPA for enrolled students; no remarketing on protected characteristics.

## Automotive, real estate, and general local services
- Fair Housing Act limits targeting for housing ads (Google's housing, employment, and credit policy restricts location, age, gender, and interest targeting in the US); real estate license disclosures; automotive pricing and financing disclosures (state and federal).

## Always, in every vertical
- Call recording consent by state; a static vendor number for call assets; recordings stay with the sender's client unless a data agreement says otherwise.
- FTC Endorsement Guides and the reviews rule for any testimonial or rating claim.
- Trademark use in ad copy: product brands only when the client actually sells or uses them; competitor names never in ad text.
- Google's conversion-volume guidance for automated bidding, so the strategy plans bidding by phase.
