# Worked example: Dr. David Hartigan (Twin Cities Orthopedics) for Humanus Marketing

How the generic workflow specializes to a real prospect. Nothing here hardcodes healthcare into the workflow; the vertical only switches on the regulated-industries module.

## Normalized inputs
- Sender: Humanus Marketing (Kristina Hansen, founder; contracting entity to confirm). Hidden party: Samprand (never in client-facing files). Reviewer: James Hamm.
- Prospect: Dr. David Hartigan, orthopedic surgeon, Twin Cities Orthopedics; contact Stephanie; several firms asked to propose.
- Services: computer-navigated total hip replacement; robotic-assisted total knee replacement; hip arthroscopy for labral tears; ACL reconstruction; meniscus repair; rotator cuff repair.
- Seed searches: knee replacement doctor/surgeon; hip replacement doctor/surgeon; hip preservation doctor/surgeon; treatment for hip or knee arthritis; labral repair surgeon; hip impingement surgeon; rotator cuff repair surgeon; ACL reconstruction surgeon.
- Geography: Plymouth, Maple Grove, Edina, and the broader Twin Cities.
- Objective: qualified patient inquiries and surgical consultations, not clicks.
- Requested items: strategy, monthly spend, management and setup fees, expected CPC and cost per lead, landing pages, conversion tracking, reporting, contract and cancellation terms, conflict disclosure, market exclusivity discussion.
- Essential input missing: Keyword Planner data for the Twin Cities. Commercial inputs missing: all of them (Humanus decisions).

## Research that the workflow can do
Verify the surgeon's affiliation, clinics, training, and which services his pages emphasize (hip arthroscopy and anterior outpatient hip replacement were verifiable; robotic knee and navigated hip were not, so they become kickoff confirmations). Cluster the seeds into hip replacement, knee replacement, hip preservation, and sports (ACL, meniscus, rotator cuff). Note competitors advertising robotic systems in the priority suburbs. Note that group pages name other surgeons and that the personal site has no knee page. Flag healthcare tracking posture in two sentences.

## The data request (this is the step the flawed run skipped)
Ask Kristina to run Keyword Planner from her Google Ads account: paste the keyword list; three runs (Plymouth, Maple Grove, Edina; Minneapolis-St. Paul DMA; Minnesota for hip preservation only); last 12 months; download historical metrics with top-of-page bids. Until it arrives, any budget is provisional and says so.

## Entry budget
With the export: coverage math over the priority clusters (hip replacement, knee replacement, a small hip preservation line) at a 40% to 65% impression share gives the media range; recommend the rounded midpoint as one figure and say what it covers and what would scale it. Without the export: an order-of-magnitude provisional range, labeled, with the request attached. No 9.37 inquiries; no $3,000/$6,000/$9,000 ladder unless the coverage differences justify alternatives.

## Proposal (2 to 4 pages)
Understanding; findings (cluster table with demand and CPC ranges, two market observations, the limitations line); starting approach (hip and knee replacement plus hip preservation first, sports later; priority suburbs weighted inside the metro; hip preservation reaches farther); entry budget; measurement (qualified inquiries and consultations counted by the practice, calls and forms tracked without contents, privacy-safe posture); landing pages (dedicated pages recommended, basic tracking); fees and terms as [KRISTINA TO SET] and [KRISTINA TO CONFIRM] placeholders with the two conflict drafts and the exclusivity options; next step (a 30-minute call).

## Humanus decisions (placeholders, never invented)
Fees and setup fee; contract term and cancellation; conflict answer; exclusivity position (with the pipeline trade-off noted in the internal note); BAA readiness if Humanus will touch call recordings or form contents.

## Stage 2, if the practice advances
Campaign structure, ad copy, landing-page briefs, HIPAA tracking architecture (vendor BAAs, number insertion, data-flow table, offline import decision), reporting, gates, and the launch timeline.
