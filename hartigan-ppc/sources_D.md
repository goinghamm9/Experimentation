## Assumptions

- Assumption: D50 | Launch clusters are hip replacement, knee replacement, and hip preservation (small); ACL, meniscus, and rotator cuff run as one sports medicine campaign from the day 60 gate or the Growth tier (O13 confirmed, no adjustment). | Demand ceilings (B63) show meniscus and rotator cuff cannot carry campaigns; hip preservation is his verified differentiator (A1, A5, A6); joint replacement carries the demand and the only known case value (A20); an entry budget split six ways would not produce a readable search term report per cluster by day 30. | A Keyword Planner export showing sports surgeon-seeking volume well above the model, or the practice ranking sports procedures above joint replacement.
- Assumption: D51 | Base demand ceiling per cluster is the geometric mean of Agent B's low and high, rounded to two significant figures; Tier 3 pools use the same rule, with hip preservation Tier 3 high scaled to the O11 ring by the B53 ratio. | B's band is the modeled point times 0.6 and 1.5 (B55); the square root of 0.6 x 1.5 is 0.95, so the geometric mean sits within 5% of B's modeled point without inventing a new figure. | A Keyword Planner 12-month average (B64).
- Assumption: D52 | Phrase-match query expansion factor: low 1.0, base 1.25, high 1.5 eligible searches per listed keyword search. | Phrase match serves for queries that include the meaning of the keyword with words before or after, and close variants add synonyms and same-intent queries (D12), so eligible impressions exceed the keyword list's volume; the low case assumes no expansion beyond the list; the range is a modeling judgment. | The day 30 impression share and search term reports, which show eligible impressions directly (D13).
- Assumption: D53 | Achievable search impression share on Tier 1 and Tier 2 terms if budget allowed: low 40%, base 60%, high 75%. | A surgeon-level advertiser bidding against hospital systems and large groups (B61) will not reach the 60% to 80% that blog benchmarks give high-intent non-brand campaigns (D11) at the low end; the high case assumes strong ad rank on tight terms. | Observed impression share and lost-to-rank after 30 days (D13).
- Assumption: D54 | Search CTR by budget tier: Pilot 6% / 8% / 11%; Growth 5.5% / 7.5% / 10%; Full coverage 5% / 7% / 9%. | The national physicians and surgeons CTR is 6.61% (B1); the Pilot runs the tightest surgeon-seeking and procedure terms so its blend sits above the category, while Growth and Full add broader terms and Tier 3 tests that lower the blend. Not a Twin Cities figure. | Observed CTR by ad group after 30 days.
- Assumption: D55 | Tier 3 inclusion share by budget tier: Pilot 0%, Growth 20%, Full coverage 35% of Tier 3 searches added to the eligible pool, held equal across cases. | Tier 3 condition research terms run as capped exact-match tests from Growth (02_keyword_summary.md); their clicks are discounted for AI Overviews (B65) and lower intent, so only a fraction of their volume is treated as eligible. | Tier 3 test results and the practice's appetite for cost and condition pages.
- Assumption: D56 | Click to contact rate (call of 60 seconds or more, or form): low 6%, base 10%, high 14%. | National physicians and surgeons conversion rates are 12.43% (B1, 2026) and 11.62% (B2, 2025); the base is set below them because surgical searches carry a longer decision, the primary conversion counts only 60-second calls and forms (C13), and new landing pages start unproven. | Observed conversion rate after tracking is verified at week 2, and the practice's answer rate (C51).
- Assumption: D57 | Contact to qualified inquiry rate (rubric C50): low 30%, base 45%, high 60%. | No orthopedic benchmark exists. Invoca reports 42% of healthcare calls book on the call (D9), which requires a qualified caller; agency claims of about 1 in 9 inquiries converting (D14, low reliability) and Agent C's expectation that ad-platform counts exceed practice counts (C57) set the pessimistic end. | The NQ-code tally from intake after month one.
- Assumption: D58 | Qualified inquiry to consult booked rate: low 50%, base 65%, high 80%. | A qualified inquiry has already passed the new-patient, area, condition, and insurance screen, so booking should exceed the all-calls rate of 42% (D9); missed calls and callback lag (C51) hold the low end down. | Practice-reported booked consults by month.
- Assumption: D59 | Consult booked to consult attended rate: low 75%, base 85%, high 92%. | Orthopedic clinic no-show studies range from 6.9% (arthroplasty) to 25.8% (hand), 11.5% overall (D10); new patients arriving from ads with no prior relationship may no-show more than established patients. | Practice-reported attended consults by month.
