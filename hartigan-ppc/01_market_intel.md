## 6. Geography for hip preservation

Evidence. Twin Cities hip arthroscopists surfaced: five at TCO [A1, A51, A52], two at Allina [A62, A63], one at Mayo Minneapolis [A58], one at TRIA [A57]. Rochester has Mayo's hip preservation clinic [A59]. Western Wisconsin has two in Eau Claire [A67, A68] and St. Croix Orthopaedics in Hudson [A66]. No fellowship-trained hip preservation surgeon surfaced in St. Cloud or Duluth [A70]. Drives: St. Cloud to Maple Grove 54 mi; Hudson to Edina 37 mi; Rochester to Edina 85 mi [A71]. The Minneapolis-St. Paul DMA already spans 58 counties, including Stearns and six western Wisconsin counties [A72]; TCO has clinics in Hudson and New Richmond [A73].

Recommendation: give the hip preservation cluster a wider geography than the other clusters, but not the whole state. Target the metro plus a ring of roughly 60 miles from Maple Grove, which covers the St. Cloud corridor, the western exurbs, and St. Croix, Pierce, and Polk counties in Wisconsin. Exclude Olmsted County (Mayo's home market), Duluth, and Eau Claire, which have their own hip arthroscopists two hours away. Keep joint replacement, knee, and shoulder clusters inside the metro. Expand statewide only if consults arrive from the ring [A82]. Confidence: medium; the ring adds reach at low cost, and outstate demand is thin and unmeasured.
