Exclude Olmsted County (Mayo's home market), Duluth, and Eau Claire, which have their own hip arthroscopists two hours away. Keep the other clusters inside the metro. Expand statewide only if consults arrive from the ring [A82]. Confidence: medium; the ring adds reach at low cost, and outstate demand is thin and unmeasured.
